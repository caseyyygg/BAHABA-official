Delete your BANTALA account

To request deletion without signing in, enter the email address on your BANTALA account. We will email a one-time confirmation link. Your account is not deleted until you open that link. The link expires after 24 hours.

Confirming will permanently delete your account and its saved account data, including registered push devices. Public-safety or administrative report records may be retained where needed for public safety and record integrity.
