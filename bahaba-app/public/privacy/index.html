BANTALA Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

BANTALA helps residents view flood-related reports, alerts, maps, and local advisories. This policy explains what information the BANTALA app processes and how to request its deletion.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, username, password hash, and account and verification status when you create an account. Passwords are stored as password hashes, not readable passwords.
  • Location and preferences: your selected and preferred local areas. If you enable location sharing and grant device permission, the app may use your device's location to identify the relevant area. Location sharing is optional and can be turned off in Settings.
  • App settings: choices such as notification preferences and display settings.
  • Push notifications: if enabled on Android, a device registration token is associated with your account so the service can deliver relevant flood alerts and advisories.
  • Service requests: an email address and a temporary, protected confirmation token when you request account deletion through this website.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to authenticate accounts, provide location-relevant reports and announcements, deliver requested notifications, maintain security, and respond to account-deletion requests. We do not sell personal information.

The app communicates with BANTALA's service infrastructure to store account and service data. When used, device push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. If you request an address lookup from device coordinates, the lookup is provided by BigDataCloud. Online maps use map and geographic data services, including OpenStreetMap contributors. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own privacy terms.

Flood reports and advisories may be visible to app users or authorized local administrators to support public-safety information. Report and administrative records may be retained when needed for public safety and record integrity; they are not used to keep a deleted account active.

Storage and security

Account information is held by BANTALA's service infrastructure while the account is active. Network requests to the production service use HTTPS. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and account deletion

You can turn off device location sharing and notifications in app Settings. To delete your account in the app, sign in, open Settings → Delete account, enter your current password, and confirm. To request deletion without signing in to the app, use the BANTALA account-deletion page and confirm the link sent to your account email. Account deletion removes the account and its registered push devices. Public-safety or administrative report records may be retained as described above.

Children

BANTALA is a general community information service and is not designed specifically for children. Do not provide another person's personal information without permission.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact details on BANTALA's Google Play Store listing.
